    exposome-schema

    Comprehensive exposome schema for integrating chemical exposures, environmental factors, dietary data, toxicology databases, and health outcomes with support for Adverse Outcome Pathways (AOPs) and multi-granularity measurements

    URI: https://w3id.org/diatomsRcool/exposome-schema

    Name: exposome-schema
